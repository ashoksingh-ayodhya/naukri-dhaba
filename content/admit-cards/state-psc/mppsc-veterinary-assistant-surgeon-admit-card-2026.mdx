The Madhya Pradesh Public Service Commission (MPPSC) has released the MPPSC Veterinary Assistant Surgeon Admit Card 2026 for candidates who applied against Advertisement No. 04/2026 for the posts of Assistant Director / Veterinary Assistant Surgeon / Veterinary Extension Officer under the Animal Husbandry and Dairy Department, Government of Madhya Pradesh. As per the official exam notice (Notice No. 01/04/2026, dated 29.05.2026), the admit card has been made available on the Commission's website from 26 July 2026, ahead of the written examination scheduled on 2 August 2026.

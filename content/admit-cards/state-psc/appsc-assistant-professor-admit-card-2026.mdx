The Arunachal Pradesh Public Service Commission (APPSC) has released the Admit Card for the Viva-Voce/Interview round of the Assistant Professor Examination-2026 vide Notification No. PSC-R(B)/01/2025/Itanagar dated 5th August 2026. The admit card is meant for candidates in Anthropology, Sociology, Chemistry, Physics, and Mathematics who applied for the post of Assistant Professor under the Department of Education, Government of Arunachal Pradesh. Candidates can download their admit card from 7:00 PM on 5th August 2026 to 2:30 PM on 14th August 2026.

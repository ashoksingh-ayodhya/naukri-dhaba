The Staff Selection Commission (SSC) has issued an official notice for the Physical Standard Test (PST) Admit Card 2026 for candidates recommended in the Combined Hindi Translators (CHT) Examination 2025, for the post of Sub-Inspector (Hindi Translator) in the Central Reserve Police Force (CRPF). The admit card available for download from 7th August 2026 on the SSC Eastern Region website. The PST itself is scheduled for 17th August 2026.

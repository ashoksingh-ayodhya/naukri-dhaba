UPSSSC AGTA Admit Card 2026: The Uttar Pradesh Subordinate Services Selection Commission (UPSSSC) has released the admit card for the Agriculture Technical Assistant (AGTA) Group C written examination on 5 August 2026. This admit card is for the recruitment against Advertisement No. 06-Exam/2026, covering a total of 2759 vacancies under the Agriculture Department. The written exam is scheduled for 9 August 2026. Candidates who applied and appeared in UPSSSC PET 2025 can download their admit card from the official website.

A total of **2759** posts are available for this recruitment.

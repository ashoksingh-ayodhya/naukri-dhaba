UPSC CMS Admit Card 2026: The Union Public Service Commission (UPSC) has released the Admit Card for the Combined Medical Services Examination (CMSE) 2026 on the official website upsc.gov.in. Candidates who applied for the 1,358 Medical Officer vacancies can now download their hall ticket using their Registration ID (RID) or Roll Number along with their Date of Birth. The CMS 2026 written exam is scheduled for 2 August 2026.

A total of **1358** posts are available for this recruitment.

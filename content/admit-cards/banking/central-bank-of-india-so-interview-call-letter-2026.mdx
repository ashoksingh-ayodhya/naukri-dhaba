Central Bank of India (CBI) has released the interview call letter for the Recruitment Process for Selection of Officers in Specialist Category in Risk Management Streams. Candidates who cleared the written examination can now download their interview call letter online from the official portal, ibpsreg.ibps.in. The interview call letter download window is open from 23 July 2026 to 30 July 2026, and the personal interview is scheduled for 30 July 2026.

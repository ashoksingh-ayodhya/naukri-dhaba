The Meghalaya Public Service Commission (MPSC) has released the admit card for the Lower Division Assistant (LDA) Screening Test under the Heads of Department (July 2025) recruitment cycle. Candidates who applied for the 71 LDA vacancies can download their hall ticket from the official MPSC Meghalaya website. The written examination is scheduled for 22nd August 2026.

A total of **71** posts are available for this recruitment.

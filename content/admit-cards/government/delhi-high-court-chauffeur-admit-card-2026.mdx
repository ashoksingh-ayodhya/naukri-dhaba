The High Court of Delhi has issued a notice for the download of the Stage-II (Skill Test) admit card for the Chauffeur (Open) Examination 2025. This recruitment was originally advertised by the Delhi Subordinate Services Selection Board (DSSSB) on behalf of the Delhi High Court under Advertisement No. 04/2025, covering the posts of Chauffeur (Driver) and Despatch Rider-cum-Process Server. Candidates who cleared Stage-I (the Preliminary Examination) can now download their Stage-II Skill Test admit card from the official portal. The admit card was released on 5 August 2026.

CGSSB Tracer (Civil) Admit Card 2026 has been announced by the Chhattisgarh Staff Selection Board (CGSSB, formerly Vyapam) for the written recruitment exam WRDT26, held for Tracer(Civil) - also referred to as Tracer (Civil) - posts under the Water Resources Department, Nava Raipur. Candidates can download their admit card from 27 July 2026 onward for the exam scheduled on 2 August 2026. This page covers the official download link, exam schedule, required documents, and exam-day instructions as issued in the official notice dated 26 July 2026.

The Space Applications Centre (SAC), Indian Space Research Organisation (ISRO) has released the ISRO SAC Admit Card 2026 on 27th July 2026 for the Scientist/Engineer 'SC' and 'SD' posts under Advertisement No. SAC:01:2026. Candidates who applied online between 23rd January 2026 and 12th February 2026 can now download their hall ticket using their registration number and password or date of birth. The Computer-Based Test (CBT) is scheduled for 30th July 2026 (Thursday). This page explains how to download the ISRO SAC Admit Card, what details it contains, and what to do if you spot an error.

A total of **49** posts are available for this recruitment.

The Railway Recruitment Board (RRB) has started releasing the RRB Group D Admit Card 2026 for the Level-1 (Group D) Computer Based Test under CEN 09/2025. The admit card is being issued in phases, roughly four days before each candidate's scheduled exam date, on the official RRB candidate login portal. Candidates can download their hall ticket using their Registration Number and Date of Birth or Password. This article covers the direct download link, the exam-date-wise release schedule, the steps to download, documents to carry, and answers to common questions.

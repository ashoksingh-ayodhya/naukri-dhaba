The Uttar Pradesh Education Service Selection Commission (UPESSC) has released the UP TGT 2026 Special Document Verification (DV) admit card for candidates who missed the earlier document verification round despite being included in the merit list. Eligible candidates can download the DV call letter from the official website, upessc.up.gov.in, and appear for verification on August 10, 2026.

A total of **3539** posts are available for this recruitment.

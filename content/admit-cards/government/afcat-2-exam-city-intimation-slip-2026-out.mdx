The AFCAT 2 2026 city intimation slip has been released on 29th July 2026 at the official website, afcat.edcil.co.in. Candidates appearing for the AFCAT 02/2026 exam on 8th August 2026 can now check their allotted exam city through the official login window. The Indian Air Force (IAF) has also sent the city details to candidates on their registered email ID and mobile number. The AFCAT admit card 2026 will be released separately at a later date. Candidates are advised to keep the city intimation slip safe until the exam concludes, and must carry the admit card along with a valid photo ID on exam day.

Railway Recruitment Board (RRB) has released the RRB ALP CBT 2 Admit Card 2026 on 24 July 2026 at rrbcdg.gov.in and other regional RRB websites for candidates who qualified CBT 1 under CEN No. 01/2025. The admit card is for 9,970 (revised to 10,970) Assistant Loco Pilot (ALP) posts, and the CBT 2 exam is scheduled for 28 July 2026. Candidates can download the hall ticket using their registration number and password or date of birth.

A total of **9970** posts are available for this recruitment.

Kerala Pareeksha Bhavan has released the KTET Hall Ticket 2026 for the K-TET February 2026 session on the official website, ktet.kerala.gov.in. The download link went live on 28 July 2026, as confirmed by the "KTET February 2026 Admit Card Published" notice on the official homepage. This hall ticket is for the Kerala Teacher Eligibility Test being held on 8 and 9 August 2026. Registered candidates can download their admit card by logging in with their application number and application ID.

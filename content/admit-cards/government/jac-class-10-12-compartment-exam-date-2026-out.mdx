The Jharkhand Academic Council (JAC) has announced the compartment (supplementary) exam schedule for Class 10 students for 2026. According to the official update, the JAC Class 10 compartment exam 2026 will be held from August 7 to August 12, 2026. As of now, JAC has not released an official, confirmed date sheet for the Class 12 compartment exam 2026; multiple education portals report it is expected in August 2026, but this is not yet officially confirmed.


## How to Apply

1. Students apply for the compartment exam through their respective schools; there is no separate individual online portal for direct student registration.
2. The application is generally submitted along with a photocopy of the relevant mark sheet, as required by the school.
3. For Class 10, the last date to apply with a late fee was July 13, 2026.
4. For Class 10, hall tickets are scheduled to be released on July 30, 2026, and will be distributed to students by their respective schools.
5. A separate admit card timeline for the Class 12 compartment exam has not yet been officially announced.
6. Carry the official hall ticket to the exam centre; entry without it may not be permitted.
7. Reach the exam centre well before the reporting time on each exam day.
8. Regularly check the official JAC website for updates, since compartment exam schedules, admit card dates, and instructions can be revised by the board.

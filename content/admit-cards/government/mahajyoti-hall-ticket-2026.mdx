The Mahatma Jyotiba Phule Research and Training Institute (MahaJyoti) has released the MahaJyoti Hall Ticket 2026 on its official website, mahajyoti.org.in. This admit card is for candidates appearing in MahaJyoti's screening/selection test for its free competitive exam training programs, covering categories such as TAIT, UGC NET/CSIR/MH-SET, MBA CAT/CMAT-CET, GRE/TOEFL/IELTS & Foreign Languages, and SSC/IBPS/RRB (Banking/LIC/AAO), Railway Recruitment, and Military/Police training tracks. The exam will be held from 8 to 10 August 2026 across multiple shifts. Candidates can log in using their Aadhaar Number and Mobile Number to download the hall ticket.

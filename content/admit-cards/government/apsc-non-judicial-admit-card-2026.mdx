The Assam Public Service Commission (APSC) has opened admit card download for the Screening Test (OMR-based) against Advertisement No. 33/2025 dated 12-12-2025, issued for the recruitment of Member posts in the Assam State Consumer Disputes Redressal Commission (Non-Judicial) and the District Consumer Disputes Redressal Commissions. Candidates who applied online can download their admit card using their application number through the official APSC recruitment portal.

A total of **35** posts are available for this recruitment.

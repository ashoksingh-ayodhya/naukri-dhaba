The Haryana Public Service Commission (HPSC) has announced that admit cards for the Combined Subject Knowledge Test for the posts of Assistant District Attorney in the Prosecution Department, Haryana (Advt. No. 18/2025) and Superintendent (Legal) in the Law and Legislative Department, Haryana (Advt. No. 01/2025) will be available for download from 3 August 2026 onwards at the official website hpsc.gov.in. The Subject Knowledge Test for both posts is scheduled for 9 August 2026.

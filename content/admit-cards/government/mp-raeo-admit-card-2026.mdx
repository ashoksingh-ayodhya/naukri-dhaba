The MP RAEO Admit Card 2026 for the Rural/Agriculture Extension Officer (Group 2, Sub-Group 1) Computer-Based Test has will be released by the Madhya Pradesh Employees Selection Board (MPESB). The MPESB AEO/RAEO exam, originally scheduled for 2 August 2026, has been revised by the board to 5 August 2026. Candidates who applied online between 3 and 17 July 2026 for the 2,784 RAEO vacancies will be able to download their hall ticket from the official website, esb.mp.gov.in, once MPESB activates the admit card link.

A total of **2784** posts are available for this recruitment.

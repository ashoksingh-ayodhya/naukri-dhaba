Short Information : Madhya Pradesh Employees Selection Board (MPESB) has released the Revised Exam Date for the post of Group-2 Sub Group -1 Krashi Vistar Adhikari (Agriculture Extension Officer AEO). This recruitment has been issued for 2784 posts. The online application process for MPESB Krashi Vistar Adhikari Recruitment 2026 were accepted from 03 July 2026, to 17 July 2026. The Exam is going to conducted on 17 September 2026. Candidates must check the complete details for MPESB Krashi Vistar Adhikari Revised Exam Date 2026, which are given below.

A total of **122** posts are available for this recruitment.
Candidates must have 1126 as minimum educational qualification.

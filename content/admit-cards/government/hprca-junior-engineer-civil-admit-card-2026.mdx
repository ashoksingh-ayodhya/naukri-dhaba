The Himachal Pradesh Rajya Chayan Ayog (HPRCA), Hamirpur has released the admit card for the Junior Engineer (Civil) post, advertised under Post Code 26016. The Computer Based Test (CBT) for this post is scheduled for 9 August 2026, and will be conducted in three shifts on the same day. Candidates can download their admit card from the official HPRCA portal using their Registration ID and date of birth.

A total of **149** posts are available for this recruitment.

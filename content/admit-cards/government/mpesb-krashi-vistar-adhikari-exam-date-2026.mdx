Madhya Pradesh Employees Selection Board (MPESB), Bhopal has revised the exam date for the Group-2 Sub Group-1 (Krashi Vistar Adhikari / Agriculture Extension Officer) Recruitment Exam 2026. The computer-based test, originally scheduled for 2 August 2026, will now be held from 17 September 2026 (Thursday) onwards, in two shifts of three hours each. This article covers the updated exam schedule, admit card status, exam cities, and documents required at the centre, based on the official notification and the revised exam date change notice.

A total of **2784** posts are available for this recruitment.

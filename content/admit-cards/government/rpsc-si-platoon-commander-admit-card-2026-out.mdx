RPSC SI Admit Card 2026: The Rajasthan Public Service Commission (RPSC) has released the admit card for the Sub Inspector (SI) / Platoon Commander Combined Competitive Examination 2025 on 29 July 2026 on the official Rajasthan State Recruitment Portal. This recruitment drive covers 1,015 vacancies across Sub Inspector and Platoon Commander posts in Rajasthan Police. Candidates who cleared the earlier stages of this recruitment can now download their admit card using their Application Number and Date of Birth. This admit card follows the written examination (April 2026), result (June 2026), and interview (July 2026) stages already completed in this recruitment cycle; candidates should select the correct category on the portal to download the admit card applicable to their current stage.

A total of **1015** posts are available for this recruitment.

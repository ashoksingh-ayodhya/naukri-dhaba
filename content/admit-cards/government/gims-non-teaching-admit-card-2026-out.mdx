The Government Institute of Medical Sciences (GIMS), Greater Noida has released the GIMS Non-Teaching Admit Card 2026 for the Clerical Cadre posts of Record Keeper and Store Keeper. Candidates who applied under Advertisement No. GIMS/2026/Manpower/NT-02/07 can download their hall ticket by logging in with their Registration Number and Password. The GIMS Clerical Cadre Computer-Based Test (CBT) will be held on 7th August 2026.

A total of **33** posts are available for this recruitment.

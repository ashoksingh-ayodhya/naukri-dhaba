The Punjab State Power Corporation Limited (PSPCL) has released the PSPCL JE Admit Card 2026 for the post of Junior Engineer/Electrical against advertisement CRA 316/26. Candidates who applied for this recruitment can now download their hall ticket from the official website using their application number and date of birth. The PSPCL JE Online Examination is scheduled for 31st July 2026 in multiple shifts.

Candidates must have Diploma/B.E./B.Tech in Electrical Engineering as minimum educational qualification.

Indian Overseas Bank (IOB) released the IOB Apprentice Admit Card 2026 on 29th July 2026 for candidates appearing in the recruitment exam for 750 Apprentice posts. The online examination is scheduled for 2nd August 2026. The admit card carries the candidate's roll number, exam centre details, reporting time, and exam-day instructions. Candidates who applied for the IOB Apprentice Recruitment 2026 must download and carry a printed copy of the call letter to the exam, as it is mandatory for entry.

A total of **750** posts are available for this recruitment.

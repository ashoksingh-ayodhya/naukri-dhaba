The Karnataka Examinations Authority (KEA) has activated the admit card download link for the Department of Agriculture, Government of Karnataka recruitment to the posts of Agricultural Officer (AO) and Assistant Agricultural Officer (AAO) under the Kalyana Karnataka (KK) cadre on 02-08-2026. The KK cadre written examination is scheduled for 8 August 2026 (Saturday), as per the revised exam schedule issued by KEA on 19 July 2026. Candidates who applied under Advt. No. EDI/KEA/09/SEV/2026 (also referenced as EDI/KEA/09/KK/2026) dated 30.05.2026 can download their admit card from the official KEA exam portal using their registered login credentials.

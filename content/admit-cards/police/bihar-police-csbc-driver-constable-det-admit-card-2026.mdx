Short Information : Central Selection Board of Constable (CSBC) will release the DET Admit Card for the post of Driver Constables. This recruitment was released for 4361 posts. Applications for the Bihar Police CSBC Driver Constable Recruitment 2025 were accepted from July 21, 2025, to August 20, 2025. The PET examination was held on 12 March 2026. Candidates who appeared for this examination can download their Bihar Police CSBC Driver Constable DET Admit Card 2026 Soon from the link given below.

A total of **24** posts are available for this recruitment.
Candidates must have Height : (All Category Applicants) – 155 Cms; Weight : 48 KG (Min); Race : 01 KM in 07 Minutes; Long Jump : 07 Feet (Min); High Jump : 02 Feet 06 Inches (Min); Shot Put : 12 Pounds Shot minimum 08 Feet Throw as minimum educational qualification.

The Central Selection Board of Constable (CSBC) will release the Bihar Police Driver Constable DET Admit Card 2026 on 10 August 2026 on its official website, csbc.bihar.gov.in. The admit card is for the Driving Efficiency Test (DET), scheduled to be held on 24 August 2026, for candidates who have qualified the Physical Efficiency Test (PET) under Advertisement No. 02/2025 for 4,361 Driver Constable posts. Candidates will be able to download the admit card by logging in with their Registration Number, Enrollment Number, or Date of Birth.

A total of **4361** posts are available for this recruitment.

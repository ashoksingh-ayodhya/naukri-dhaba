The Maharashtra Talathi Syllabus 2026 covers Marathi, English, General Knowledge, Mathematics (Arithmetic) and General Intelligence (Reasoning). The exam is conducted by the Maharashtra Revenue and Forest Department to recruit candidates for the post of Talathi, who manages village-level land records and revenue work. Since the written exam is the only stage that decides selection, candidates must study the syllabus and exam pattern together to plan their preparation efficiently. This article covers the subject-wise Talathi syllabus, exam pattern, selection process, and preparation tips based on the official syllabus and the previously conducted Talathi exam pattern.

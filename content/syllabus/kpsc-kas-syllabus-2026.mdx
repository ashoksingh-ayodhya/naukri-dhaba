The Karnataka Public Service Commission (KPSC) released the KPSC KAS (Karnataka Administrative Service) Gazetted Probationers Notification 2026 on 31 July 2026 for 319 vacancies. This article covers the complete KPSC KAS Syllabus 2026 and Exam Pattern as detailed in the official notification, including the Prelims paper-wise topics, the Mains paper structure, marking scheme and selection process. Since the KAS Prelims and Mains exams test candidates on overlapping subject areas, understanding the syllabus and exam pattern together is essential for building an effective KPSC KAS preparation strategy.

A total of **319** posts are available for this recruitment.

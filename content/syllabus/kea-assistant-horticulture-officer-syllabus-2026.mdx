The Directorate of Horticulture, Karnataka has released the Assistant Horticulture Officer Syllabus 2026 for candidates preparing for the recruitment exam. The syllabus is approved by the Competitive Examination Syllabus Committee and covers ten major subject areas ranging from Fruit Science and Vegetable Science to Soil Science, Agricultural Engineering, and Social and Allied Sciences. Candidates applying for this post should go through the complete subject-wise syllabus given below to plan an effective preparation strategy. This article covers the eligibility qualification, the full topic-wise syllabus for every subject, and answers to common questions asked by candidates.

Candidates must have Graduation in Horticulture from a University established by law in India and accredited by ICAR as minimum educational qualification.

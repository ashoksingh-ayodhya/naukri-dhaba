The Odisha Sub-ordinate Staff Selection Commission (OSSSC) conducts the Pharmacist recruitment exam to fill vacancies in the state health department. The OSSSC Pharmacist Syllabus 2026 lists the subjects and topics candidates must prepare, while the OSSSC Pharmacist Exam Pattern explains the marking scheme, sections, and duration of the written test. Since the official 2026 notification has not been released yet, this article is based on the previous OSSSC Pharmacist exam cycle (2024 recruitment). Candidates should verify every detail against the latest OSSSC notification once it is published, and treat figures here as indicative rather than final.

The Society for Centralized Recruitment of Staff in Subordinate Courts (S.S.S.C.), functioning under the Punjab and Haryana High Court, has released the Haryana Court Clerk Syllabus 2026 along with the recruitment notification for 1265 vacancies. Candidates preparing for the exam must go through the syllabus and exam pattern together to build an effective preparation strategy, since the written exam tests General Knowledge and English Composition through both objective and subjective questions.

A total of **1265** posts are available for this recruitment.

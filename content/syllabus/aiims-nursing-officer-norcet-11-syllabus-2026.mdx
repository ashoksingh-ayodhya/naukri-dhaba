The All India Institute of Medical Sciences (AIIMS), New Delhi has released the AIIMS Nursing Officer Syllabus 2026 along with the exam pattern for NORCET-11, vide Notice No.103/2026 dated 24.07.2026. AIIMS conducts the Nursing Officer Recruitment Common Eligibility Test (NORCET) to fill Nursing Officer vacancies across AIIMS New Delhi and other participating AIIMS institutes. Since NORCET is held in two stages with different marks, duration and qualifying criteria, candidates need to understand the syllabus and exam pattern together to plan their preparation effectively. This article covers the official exam pattern, selection process, marking scheme and preparation tips for NORCET-11, based strictly on the official notification.

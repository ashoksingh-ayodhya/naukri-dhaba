The OICL Assistant Syllabus and Exam Pattern is the starting point for anyone preparing for the Oriental Insurance Company Limited (OICL) Assistant recruitment exam. Since the OICL Assistant 2026 notification has not been released yet, this article is based on the OICL Assistant Recruitment 2025 notification (Ref. No: OICL/Rect./2025), the most recent official notification issued by OICL for 500 Assistant (Class III) vacancies. Knowing the exam pattern and subject-wise syllabus in advance helps candidates plan their preparation for the Prelims, Mains, and Regional Language Test stages.

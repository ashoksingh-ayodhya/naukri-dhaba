The State Bank of India (SBI) has released the notification for recruitment of Junior Associates (Customer Support & Sales) under a Special Recruitment Drive for SC/ST/OBC Backlog Vacancies (Advertisement No. CRPD/CR/SPLDRIVE/2026-27/16). Online registration is open from 7 August 2026 to 27 August 2026. This article explains the exam pattern for Prelims and Mains, the Local Language Proficiency Test, marking scheme, and selection process exactly as given in the official notification.

A total of **1538** posts are available for this recruitment.

## How to Apply

1. Visit the official SBI careers page: https://sbi.bank.in/web/careers/current-openings
2. Locate and open the "Special Recruitment drive for Junior Associates 2026" link.
3. Download and carefully read Advertisement No. CRPD/CR/SPLDRIVE/2026-27/16.
4. Register online with a valid email ID and mobile number.
5. Fill in the application form and upload the scanned photograph, signature, left thumb impression, and hand-written declaration.
6. Pay the application fee (if applicable) using debit card, credit card, or internet banking.
7. Submit the form and save/print the system-generated application form for your records (this printout is not to be sent to SBI).
8. Candidates can apply for vacancies in one State/UT only and can appear for the test only once under this recruitment project.
9. There is no provision for Inter-Circle or Inter-State transfer, except intra-state transfer between Delhi Circle & Lucknow Circle (Uttar Pradesh) and between Mumbai Metro Circle & Maharashtra Circle (Maharashtra), subject to the Bank's extant guidelines.
10. No changes or corrections to submitted application particulars, including category, will be entertained under any circumstances.
11. Mobile phones, smartwatches, calculators, and similar devices are strictly prohibited inside the examination premises.
12. A wait list of up to 50% of vacancies (state-category wise) will be maintained and released quarterly, valid for one year from the date of declaration of the final result.
13. SBI reserves the right to cancel the recruitment process entirely at any stage.
14. SBI has clarified it does not endorse any external coaching platform or consultancy claiming to guarantee selection; candidates should rely only on the official career portal.
15. Check SBI Junior Associates (Clerk) Syllabus and Exam pattern 2026

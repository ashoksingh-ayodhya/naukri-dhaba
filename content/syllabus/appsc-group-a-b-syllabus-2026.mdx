The Arunachal Pradesh Public Service Commission (APPSC) has notified the syllabus for the Common Combined Examination Scheme 2026, applicable to Direct Recruitment for Group-A and Group-B, Technical posts (Gazetted, Non-Ministerial/Technical). This APPSC Syllabus 2026 covers General English and General Studies, along with the overall exam pattern and selection process. Candidates preparing for posts such as Agriculture Development Officer, Veterinary Officer, Sub-Treasury Officer and other technical posts under this scheme should go through the syllabus and exam pattern together, as both determine how the written examination and final merit are structured.

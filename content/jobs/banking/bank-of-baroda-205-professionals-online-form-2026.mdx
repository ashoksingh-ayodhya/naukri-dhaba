Bank of Baroda (BoB) has released a notification, vide Advertisement No. BOB/HRM/REC/ADVT/2026/15, inviting online applications for the recruitment of human resources on a regular basis across various departments. The BoB Recruitment 2026 drive covers 206 vacancies spread over 22 posts in Digital Banking, Facility Management, Information Security, Security, Enterprise Data Management Office, and Corporate Accounts & Taxation Planning departments. Eligible candidates who are graduates or postgraduates in relevant streams such as Engineering, Computer Applications, Commerce, or hold professional qualifications like CA/ICWA can apply online through the Bank's official career page from 06.08.2026 to 26.08.2026. Selected candidates will be posted anywhere in India as per the Bank's requirement, and the recruitment process includes application scrutiny, an online test (if conducted), and psychometric test/interview.

A total of **206** posts are available for this recruitment.
Candidates must have 01.08.2026 as minimum educational qualification.

## How to Apply

1. Visit the official Bank of Baroda website at www.bankofbaroda.bank.in and go to the Career section → Current Opportunities.
2. Register online using a valid personal email ID and mobile number, which must be kept active till completion of the recruitment process.
3. Fill in the online application form carefully, upload a scanned photograph, signature, and supporting documents such as Date of Birth proof, qualification certificates, experience letters, and category certificates, as per the specifications given in the notification.
4. Verify all details before submitting, since no changes are permitted after clicking the Submit button.
5. Pay the applicable application fee online through Debit Card, Credit Card, Internet Banking, or UPI.
6. On successful payment, download and retain a printout of the e-receipt and the completed application form for future reference.
7. Only candidates willing to serve anywhere in India should apply, as selected candidates will be posted at the Bank's absolute discretion.
8. Selected candidates will be on probation for a period of 12 months.
9. Candidates selected for JMG/S-I posts must execute a service bond to serve for a minimum of 3 years or pay an amount equivalent to three months' gross salary (not exceeding Rs. 2 lakh) if they leave before completing three years.
10. Candidates selected for MMG/S-II, MMG/S-III, and SMG/S-IV posts must execute a similar service bond, with the payable amount not exceeding Rs. 3 lakh.
11. Change of category is not permitted at any stage after registration of the online application.
12. There is no reservation for Ex-servicemen in the Officers' Cadre.
13. The number of vacancies, including reserved vacancies, is provisional and may vary according to the Bank's actual requirement.
14. Not more than one application should be submitted by a candidate; in case of multiple applications, only the latest valid (completed) application will be retained.
15. Apply Online: Click here

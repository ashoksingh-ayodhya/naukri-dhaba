The Institute of Banking Personnel Selection (IBPS) has released the notification for CRP CSA-XVI, the 16th Common Recruitment Process for Customer Service Associate (CSA) posts - popularly known as IBPS Clerk Recruitment - in 11 participating Public Sector Banks. The vacancies are for the financial year 2027-28, and online registration opens on 01.08.2026.

Candidates must have A Degree (Graduation) in any discipline from a University recognised by the Government of India, or an equivalent qualification recognised by the Central Government as minimum educational qualification.

## How to Apply

1. Visit the official IBPS website www.ibps.in and open the "CRP CSA" link, then click "Click Here to Apply Online for CRP – Customer Service Associates (CRP-CSA-XVI)".
2. Click "Click Here for New Registration" and enter basic details to generate a provisional registration number and password (also sent by email/SMS).
3. Upload the required scanned documents: photograph, signature, left thumb impression, a handwritten declaration, SSC/SSLC/10th standard or equivalent certificate, and the certificate mentioned in Clause K(ix) if applicable - as per the specifications in Annexure IV of the notification.
4. Carefully fill in all sections of the online application form using the "Save and Next" facility to verify details before final submission, since most fields cannot be edited after submission.
5. Indicate the State/UT for which you want to be considered for provisional allotment - this choice is irrevocable once submitted.
6. Pay the application fee/intimation charges online through the integrated payment gateway (Debit Card/Credit Card/Internet Banking/IMPS/Wallets/UPI).
7. On successful payment, take a printout of the e-receipt and the completed application form for your records.
8. Fields such as Name, Email ID, Mobile Number, State/UT (vacancy, correspondence and permanent address), Post, and Nationality cannot be edited after submission.
9. A 2-day Edit Window is provided after the registration closing date to correct other details, for a fee of ₹200; dates for this window will be announced separately on the official website.
10. Not more than one application is allowed per candidate; in case of multiple applications, only the latest completed one is considered valid.
11. Any resulting dispute is subject to the sole jurisdiction of courts situated at Mumbai.
12. Candidates are advised to apply well before the last date to avoid last-minute technical issues.

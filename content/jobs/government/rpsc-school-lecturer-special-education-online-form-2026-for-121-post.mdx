## Eligibility for RPSC School Lecturer Special Education Online Form 2026 for 121 Post

**Educational qualification:** Post Graduation Degree with Hindi/English/History/Political Science/Sociology/Music Passed from Any Recognized University in India.; B.ED. (Special Education) or B.ED. with two years Diploma in Special Education Recognized by the Rehabilitation Council of India (RCI).; Working knowledge of Hindi wri.

**Age limit:** 21 to 40 years.

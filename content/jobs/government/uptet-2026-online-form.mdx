Uttar Pradesh Education Service Selection Commission (UPESSC) has released the UPTET 2026 Online Form notification. Online applications are accepted from 27/03/2026 to 03/05/2026. Application fee: ₹1,000 for General/OBC/EWS and ₹500 for SC/ST/PwD.

## Eligibility for UPTET 2026 Online Form

**Educational qualification:** Bachelor Degree / Master Degree with 50% Marks with B.Ed / B.Ed Special Exam Passed / Appearing (Final Year) OR; Bachelor Degree / Master Degree with BTC Training 2 Year Exam Passed OR; 10+2 Intermediate Exam with 50% Marks and B.LEd 4 Year Course OR; 10+2 Inter Exam with 50% Marks and B.SC Ed Exam.

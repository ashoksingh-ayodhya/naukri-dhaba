IndianOil Corporation Limited (IOCL), Marketing Division, has released Advertisement No. IOCL/MKTG/NR/APPR/2026-27/1 for the engagement of Technician, Graduate, and Trade Apprentices (Technical and Non-Technical) under the Apprentices Act, 1961, at locations across its Northern Region. A total of 433 apprentice seats are available across Delhi, Haryana, Punjab, Chandigarh, Himachal Pradesh, Jammu & Kashmir, Rajasthan, Uttar Pradesh, and Uttarakhand, spanning 14 discipline codes covering Technician, ITI Trade, Graduate, and Data Entry Operator apprenticeships. Candidates holding a Class X pass with relevant ITI trade certification, a recognised Diploma in Engineering, or a graduate degree in any discipline can apply online through the NAPS or NATS portal, as applicable, between 07 August 2026 (10:00 AM) and 06 September 2026 (5:00 PM). Selection is purely merit-based on qualifying marks, with no written test or interview.

Candidates must have 31 August 2026 as minimum educational qualification.

## How to Apply

1. Register as a Graduate/Technician/Trade Apprentice on the applicable portal for your state: Trade Apprentice (ITI): NAPS portal - apprenticeshipindia.gov.in Trade Apprentice-Data Entry Operator: NAPS portal - apprenticeshipindia.gov.in Technician Apprentice (Diploma): NATS portal - nats.education.gov.in Graduate Apprentice: NATS portal - nats.education.gov.in
2. Trade Apprentice (ITI): NAPS portal - apprenticeshipindia.gov.in
3. Trade Apprentice-Data Entry Operator: NAPS portal - apprenticeshipindia.gov.in
4. Technician Apprentice (Diploma): NATS portal - nats.education.gov.in
5. Graduate Apprentice: NATS portal - nats.education.gov.in
6. Ensure your profile (personal details, educational details, category, aggregate percentage) is complete and correct before applying; corrections must be made through the NAPS/NATS office.
7. Log in to the respective NATS/NAPS portal and apply for the opportunity using IOCL (MD)-Northern Region's Establishment ID: NATS – NDLSDC000005; NAPS – E05200700003.
8. After applying on the NAPS/NATS portal, also submit your details, preferred state, and three preferred locations via the Microsoft Office Form: forms.cloud.microsoft/r/pw7D2cUK7c
9. Both the NAPS/NATS application and the Microsoft Office Form must be completed by 06 September 2026, 5:00 PM. Only one Microsoft Form response is allowed - multiple submissions will not be entertained.
10. Apply for only ONE discipline code; applying for more than one will lead to rejection of the application.
11. Candidates must have a valid, active Apprentice Registration/Enrolment Number on NAPS/NATS, and 100% completion of their profile.
12. Candidates must have an active email ID and mobile number valid for at least 1 year, as all communication regarding document verification/engagement will be sent only via email/SMS.
13. Candidates must possess a PAN card, an Aadhaar card, and an active bank account with a cheque book (same account as updated on the NATS portal), seeded to Aadhaar and enabled for DBT, along with an updated APAAR ID and e-KYC verification on the NATS portal.
14. SC/ST/OBC (NCL) candidates applying for Unreserved (UR) seats will be considered only if they meet the standards applicable to General category candidates.
15. Candidates registered with a Local/State Employment Exchange/Vocational Rehabilitation Centre for PwBD must apply only through the NAPS/NATS portal.

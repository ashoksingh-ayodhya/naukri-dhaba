National Institute of Electronics and Information Technology NIELIT is a basic IT literacy program by the National Institute of Electronics and Information Technology (Government of India) to teach essential computer skills like document preparation, internet browsing, emailing, presentations, and digital services. Duration is 80 hours (30 theory + 60 practical’s). The online exam is held every Quarter , with registration typically starting on the 1st of the preceding month and the last date being the last day of As per Schedule. Read the notification for CCC Course eligibility, post information, selection procedure, Practice Test, pay scale and all other information.


## How to Apply

1. Note : Apaar ID / ABC Id is Compulsory of CCC Exam 2026
2. Candidate Can Click on the Apply Online Form
3. Fill the All Basic Details Name, Father Name, Date of Birth and Aadhar Card, Contact Details
4. Upload Candidate Passport Size Photo, Signature and Thumb Impression
5. Take A Print Out of Application Form
6. Next Month First Week You have Download Admit Card to Enter Your Application No. and DOB
7. For the latest updates on Sarkari Result, admit card, answer key, and result, always visit SarkariResult.com, the official website for Sarkari Result in India (Since 2012).

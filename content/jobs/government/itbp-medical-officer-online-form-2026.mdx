ITBP Medical Officer Recruitment 2026: Indo-Tibetan Border Police Force (ITBP) has released a recruitment short notification for Super Specialist Medical Officer, Specialist Medical Officer and Medical Officer posts. Eligible Indian male and female candidates can apply online for appointment to Group A Medical Officer posts in Central Armed Police Forces (CAPFs) under the Ministry of Home Affairs, Government of India.

Department has released an official notification for ITBP Medical Officer Recruitment 2026 - Apply Online for 282 Posts. 
The last date to apply is **08-09-2026**.
Candidates must have MBBS / Post Graduate Medical Degree / Super Speciality Qualification as minimum educational qualification.

National Insurance Company Limited (NICL), a Government of India Public Sector General Insurance Company, has invited online applications for the recruitment of 500 Assistants in Class III cadre from the open market. Online registration begins on 18th July 2026 and closes on 7th August 2026. Interested and eligible candidates can apply through the official website.

A total of **500** posts are available for this recruitment.
Candidates must have Graduation in any discipline as minimum educational qualification.

## How to Apply

1. Visit the official website nationalinsurance.nic.co.in and click "Apply Online" under the Recruitment section.
2. Register with your Name, Contact details, and Email ID to generate a Provisional Registration Number and Password.
3. Fill in the application form and use "Save and Next" to verify details before final submission.
4. Upload scanned Photograph, Signature, Left Thumb Impression, and Hand-Written Declaration as per specifications.
5. Preview and verify the entire application before clicking "Complete Registration."
6. Pay the applicable application fee online.
7. Submit the application and take a printout for future reference.
8. Candidates can apply for only one State/UT; multiple applications will render all applications invalid.
9. Candidates should ensure the name and other details match exactly with their photo identity proof.
10. Ration Card and Learner's Driving Licence are not valid ID proofs.
11. Mobile phones and other prohibited items are not allowed inside the examination centre.
12. Any request for change of examination centre, date, or session will not be entertained.
13. Newly appointed employees will be on probation for a minimum of 6 months.

Airports Authority of India (AAI), Eastern Region Apprentice Recruitment 2026-27 Notification has been released on 28-07-2026 on the official website. A total of 140 Graduate, Diploma, and ITI Apprentice vacancies have been announced through the AAI Eastern Region Apprentice Recruitment 2026-27.

A total of **140** posts are available for this recruitment.

## How to Apply

1. Graduate and Diploma candidates should visit the NATS portal at nats.education.gov.in
2. ITI candidates should visit the NAPS portal at apprenticeshipindia.gov.in
3. Register on the respective portal and search for the concerned AAI Establishment ID corresponding to the desired location (see Enrolment ID table below)
4. Fill in the required details and submit the online application
5. Applications submitted through any other mode will not be accepted
6. During the apprenticeship period, candidates will be governed by the Apprentices Act, 1961 (as amended in 1973 and till date) and the commensurate policies/rules of the organization
7. AAI reserves the right to increase, decrease, or cancel the apprenticeship engagement process without assigning any reason
8. Applications submitted through any mode other than the official NATS/NAPS portals will not be accepted

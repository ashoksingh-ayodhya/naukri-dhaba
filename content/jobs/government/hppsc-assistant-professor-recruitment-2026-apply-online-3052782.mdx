Himachal Pradesh Public Service Commission (HPPSC) has released the HPPSC Assistant Professor Recruitment 2026 - Apply Online notification. The last date to apply is 01/07/2026. Application fee: ₹600 for all categories. Pay scale: Rs.37400-67000 + (G.P. 8900) / Rs.1,29,700/- (Cell-1, Level-30).

## Eligibility for HPPSC Assistant Professor Recruitment 2026 - Apply Online

**Educational qualification:** Recognised medical qualification as per IMC Act/NMC Act 2019, Postgraduate and post-doctoral degree in concerned Super-Speciality, Minimum 3 years teaching experience after post-graduation.

**Age limit:** Maximum age: 45 years.

**Age relaxation:** Age Relaxation: Five years for SC/ST/OBC/PWD/WFF of H.P. if post reserved for the category.

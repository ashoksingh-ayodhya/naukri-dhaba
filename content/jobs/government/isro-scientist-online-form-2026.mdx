The Indian Space Research Organisation (ISRO), Department of Space, Government of India, has released the ISRO Scientist/Engineer 'SC' Recruitment 2026 Notification through the ISRO Centralised Recruitment Board (ICRB) on its official website, isro.gov.in. A total of 92 Scientist/Engineer 'SC' vacancies have been announced in Electronics, Mechanical, Computer Science, Civil, Electrical, Refrigeration & Air-Conditioning, and Architecture disciplines under the ISRO Scientist/Engineer Recruitment 2026.

A total of **92** posts are available for this recruitment.

## How to Apply

1. Applications must be submitted online at www.isro.gov.in
2. Application fee (where applicable) is payable online via the Bharatkosh portal after registration
3. Last date for online registration: 17-08-2026
4. Age is reckoned as on 17-08-2026; details of age relaxation for applicable categories are given in the website advertisement
5. Women/SC/ST and PwBD candidates are exempted from the application fee
6. Candidates must ensure receipt of the system-generated confirmation email after successful submission of application
7. Allied disciplines acceptable under each engineering branch are detailed in the FAQs on the official website
8. Government strives for workforce diversity reflecting gender balance; women candidates are encouraged to apply
9. For further details and applying, candidates must visit www.isro.gov.in

Uttarakhand Subordinate Service Selection Commission (UKSSSC) has released the Uttarakhand UKSSSC Group C Graduate Level Recruitment 2023 Apply Online for 229 Post notification for 229 posts. Online applications are accepted from 23/10/2023 to 23/11/2023. Application fee: ₹300 for General/OBC/EWS and ₹150 for SC/ST/PwD.

## Eligibility for Uttarakhand UKSSSC Group C Graduate Level Recruitment 2023 Apply Online for 229 Post

**Educational qualification:** Bachelor Degree in Any Stream in Any Recognized University in India.; Uttarakhand Domicile OR Registration in Employment Office located in Uttarakhand State OR Passed High School and Intermediate from Uttarakhand OR Spouse or your parents regular employees of the Uttarakhand.; for Post Wise Eligibil.

**Age relaxation:** Age Relaxation Extra as per UKSSSC Graduate Level Qualification of Various Departments under Group C – 2023 Recruitment Rules.

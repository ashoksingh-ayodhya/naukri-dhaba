Rashtriya Chemicals and Fertilizers Limited (RCF), a Navratna Government of India undertaking, has released the RCF Management Trainee Recruitment 2026 notification (Advertisement No. 16022026) for 94 Management Trainee posts across 14 disciplines, including Chemical, Mechanical, Electrical, Instrumentation, Civil, Fire, Information Technology, Finance, Marketing and Rajbhasha. Online registration for RCF Management Trainee Vacancy 2026 opens on 8 August 2026 and closes on 24 August 2026.

A total of **94** posts are available for this recruitment.
Candidates must have 01 February 2026 as minimum educational qualification.

## How to Apply

1. Visit the official RCF website, www.rcfltd.com, and click on "Apply Online".
2. Choose "Click here for New Registration" and enter your name, contact details, and email ID to generate a Provisional Registration Number and Password.
3. Fill in the application form, using "Save and Next" to complete it in stages if required.
4. Upload the scanned photograph, signature, left thumb impression, and handwritten declaration as per the specifications in the notification.
5. Upload other required documents (single combined PDF file per category, maximum 500 KB) as listed in the advertisement.
6. Preview and validate all details before clicking "Complete Registration".
7. Pay the application fee online, where applicable, and take a printout of the e-receipt.
8. Click "Submit" to complete the RCF Management Trainee Apply Online process.
9. Only online applications are accepted; physical application forms will not be considered.
10. Details submitted in the online application form, including correspondence address, email address, mobile number, category, post applied for, and test city, cannot be changed after submission.
11. Candidates must possess a valid personal email ID and mobile number, kept active throughout the recruitment process, as all communication will be sent electronically.
12. Candidates already employed in Central/State Government, Quasi-Government, PSUs, or Autonomous Bodies must apply through proper channel or submit a No Objection Certificate at the time of interview.
13. RCF reserves the right to cancel, restrict, enlarge, or modify the recruitment process, and to increase or decrease the number of vacancies, without prior notice.
14. Any corrigendum or update will be published only on www.rcfltd.com under "HR → Recruitment"; no separate press advertisement will be issued.

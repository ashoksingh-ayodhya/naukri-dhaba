The Andhra Pradesh Public Service Commission (APPSC) has released the Computer Proficiency Test (CPT) Result 2025 for Notification No. 12/2025 on its official portal. The result has been published district-wise, allowing candidates to check whether they have qualified by selecting their respective district from the APPSC result page. Candidates whose names or hall ticket numbers appear in the successful candidates list will be eligible to proceed to the next stage of the recruitment process, wherever applicable. Applicants are advised to download and save their result for future reference and regularly visit the official APPSC website for updates regarding certificate verification and further selection procedures.

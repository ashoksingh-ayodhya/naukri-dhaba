The Jammu and Kashmir Services Selection Board (JKSSB) has released the PST/PET shortlist for candidates who appeared in the written examination for the posts of Inspector (FPF), Wildlife Guard, FPF Guard, Field Assistant, and Social Forestry Worker (SFW) under the Forest, Ecology & Environment Department. These posts were advertised vide Notification No. 03 of 2021 and Notification No. 04 of 2022, Item Nos. 84, 85, 86, 87, 88, 89, 90, 91, 92, 185, 186, 189, 190, 592, 593, 594, 605 and 606. This notice is a shortlist for the Physical Standard Test (PST) / Physical Endurance Test (PET), also called the Walk Test it is not the final selection list. Candidates can check their roll numbers, important dates, PST/PET schedule, and download the official PDF from this page.

Candidates must have 10 August 2026 to 14 August 2026 (during office hours) as minimum educational qualification.

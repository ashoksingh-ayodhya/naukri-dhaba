South Central Railway (RRC SCR), Secunderabad, has released the Document Verification (DV) Notice and Merit List 2025-26 for Act Apprentice recruitment on 24 July 2026 under Notice No. SCR/P-HQ/RRC/111/Act.App/2025-26. A total of 6,940 candidates - 2,604 in the Main List (Annexure-I) and 4,336 in the Standby List (Annexure-II) - have been shortlisted for DV, scheduled from 10 August to 14 August 2026 across multiple venues.


## How to Apply

1. Visit the official South Central Railway website at scr.indianrailways.gov.in.
2. Navigate to "About Us" and then the "RRC-SCR" section.
3. Open the link for Act Apprentice Recruitment 2025-26 / DV Call Letter.
4. Log in using the registered email ID and password created at the time of application.
5. Download the call letter, which includes the DV venue, date, time, and medical certificate format.
6. Separately open the DV Notice PDF (Annexure-I and Annexure-II) to confirm your application number and name in the Main or Standby List.
7. Take a printout of the call letter and prepare all required documents before the scheduled DV date.
8. Candidates absent on their scheduled DV date will have their candidature cancelled without further notice; no second chance is provided.
9. A medical certificate in the prescribed format is mandatory at DV; candidature will be rejected without it.
10. Carry two self-attested photocopies of every required document along with originals.
11. Standby List candidates will be contacted only if a Main List candidate is absent or rejected.
12. Training division/unit allotment, once made, cannot be changed on request.
13. Check the registered email and SMS for call letter notification; if not received, candidates should check the RRC SCR webpage directly.
14. JKSSB Skill Test 2026 - Check Dates for Stenographer Shorthand and Junior Assistant Type Test Rescheduled
15. HPSC Superintendent Revised Result 2026 - Check Screening Test Shortlist Released

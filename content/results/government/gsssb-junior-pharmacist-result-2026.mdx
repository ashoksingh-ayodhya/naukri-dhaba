The Gujarat Subordinate Service Selection Board (GSSSB) has declared the provisional result for the Junior Pharmacist, Class-3 recruitment exam on 31 July 2026. This result is for candidates who appeared in the competitive MCQ-CBRT examination held under Advertisement No. 329/2025-26, against a total of 321 Junior Pharmacist posts. The marks obtained by each candidate are listed roll-number-wise in Annexure-A attached to the official notice.

A total of **321** posts are available for this recruitment.
Candidates must have 112 as minimum educational qualification.

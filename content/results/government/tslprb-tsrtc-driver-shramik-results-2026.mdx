The Telangana State Level Police Recruitment Board (TSLPRB), now functioning as the Telangana Police Recruitment Board (TGPRB), announced the TGSRTC Driver & Shramik Final Result 2026 on 3 August 2026. The result covers the recruitment drive for Driver and Shramik posts in the Telangana State Road Transport Corporation (TGSRTC, formerly TSRTC). A total of 1,604 candidates have been selected out of 1,743 advertised vacancies. This article explains the result, selection numbers, next steps, and required documents based on official announcements.

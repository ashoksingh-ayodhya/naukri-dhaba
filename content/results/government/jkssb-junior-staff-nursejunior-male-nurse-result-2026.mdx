The Jammu and Kashmir Services Selection Board (JKSSB) has released the JKSSB Junior Staff Nurse Result 2026 for the OMR-based written examination conducted on 28 June 2026. Candidates who appeared for the Junior Staff Nurse and Junior Male Nurse recruitment examination under Notification No. 07 of 2025 (Item Nos. 99 to 108) can now check and download their result/scoresheet PDF from the official website, jkssb.nic.in. The result has been published after the completion of the answer key objection process and includes the marks obtained by candidates in the written examination.

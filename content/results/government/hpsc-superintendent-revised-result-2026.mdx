The Haryana Public Service Commission (HPSC) has declared the revised result for the screening test conducted for the post of Superintendent (Legal) (Group-B) in the Law and Legislative Department, Haryana. The screening test was held under Advertisement No. 01/2025 and a corrigendum dated 18 May 2026. Candidates whose roll numbers appear in the official list have been provisionally shortlisted for the Subject Knowledge Test, subject to fulfillment of all other eligibility conditions.

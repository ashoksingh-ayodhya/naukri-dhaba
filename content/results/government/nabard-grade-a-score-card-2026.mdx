The National Bank for Agriculture and Rural Development (NABARD) has released the NABARD Grade A Score Card 2026 on the official website, nabard.org, on 31st July 2026. The score card is meant for candidates who appeared in the written examination for the post of Assistant Manager in Grade 'A', for the Rural Development Banking Service (RDBS), Legal, and Protocol & Security Service disciplines, against 91 vacancies. Every candidate who appeared for the exam qualified or not can download the score card using their registration number and date of birth. The NABARD Grade A Cut Off 2026 has also been released alongside the score card.

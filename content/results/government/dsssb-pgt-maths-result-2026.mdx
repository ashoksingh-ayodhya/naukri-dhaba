The Delhi Subordinate Services Selection Board (DSSSB) has released the Tier-I written exam marks and issued the e-dossier calling notice for the post of Post Graduate Teacher (PGT) Maths (Male and Female) under Post Code 825/24. This update is based on Notice No. 357 dated 31.07.2026, issued under reference number F.4(132)/DSSSB/CC-IV/2026/892-900. Candidates who appeared in the exam can check their marks through the OARS module and shortlisted candidates must upload their e-dossier within the given window.

A total of **31** posts are available for this recruitment.

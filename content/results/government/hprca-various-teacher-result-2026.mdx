The Himachal Pradesh Rajya Chayan Aayog (HPRCA), Hamirpur declared the Computer Based Screening Test (CBST) results for three Teacher recruitment posts on 24th July 2026. The results cover Teacher Physics (Post Code-26019), Teacher Biology (Post Code-26017), and Teacher Geography (Post Code-26027), all recruited on an Engagement Basis. This article summarizes the official press notes, including vacancy breakup, shortlisted candidate numbers, and the document verification schedule.

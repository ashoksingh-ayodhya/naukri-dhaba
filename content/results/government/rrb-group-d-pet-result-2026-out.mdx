North Central Railway's Railway Recruitment Cell (RRC), Prayagraj has declared the RRB Prayagraj Group D PET Result 2026 for candidates under CEN No. RRC-08/2024. The Physical Efficiency Test (PET) was conducted on 25 July 2026 at DSA Ground, Near Prayagraj Railway Station (PIN - 211001). RRC Prayagraj has released a 3-page list carrying the roll numbers of qualified candidates who cleared the PET and will now move to the next stage of selection.

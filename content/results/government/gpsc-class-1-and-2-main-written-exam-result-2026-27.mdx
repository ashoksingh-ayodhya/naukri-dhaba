The Gujarat Public Service Commission (GPSC) declared the result of the Combined Competitive (Preliminary) Examination for Advt. No. 5/2026-27 on 30 July 2026. A total of 6099 candidates have been provisionally qualified to appear in the Main Written Examination for Gujarat Administrative Service (Class-1), Gujarat Civil Service (Class 1 & 2), Gujarat State Municipal Chief Officers' Service (Class-2), and the Special Recruitment Drive for Persons with Disability (Divyang candidates). This is the Preliminary Exam result; it is not the final Main Exam result. Qualified candidates must submit a fresh Detailed Application Form (DAF) to be admitted to the Main Written Examination.


## How to Apply

1. Visit the official GPSC website (gpsc.gujarat.gov.in) or the GPSC-OJAS portal (gpsc-ojas.gujarat.gov.in).
2. Locate the DAF link for Advt. No. 5/2026-27 once released.
3. Fill in personal, educational, and experience details accurately.
4. Upload scanned photograph, signature, and required documents as specified.
5. Pay the applicable Main Exam application fee, if any, through the online payment gateway.
6. Review all entries carefully before final submission.
7. Submit the form and save/print the confirmation page.
8. This is a provisional result; final qualification is subject to eligibility verification as per Advt. No. 5/2026-27.
9. Candidates must reapply via the Detailed Application Form for the Main Written Examination qualifying prelims alone does not enroll you for Mains.
10. Regularly check the official GPSC website for the DAF schedule, fee details, and Main Exam date.
11. Retain a copy of the roll number list/result PDF for future reference.
12. For queries on individual marks or cut-off, refer to the official result PDF rather than third-party sources.

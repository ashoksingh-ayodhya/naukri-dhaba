RSSB Conductor Final Result 2026 has been declared by the Rajasthan Staff Selection Board (RSSB) on August 4, 2026. The final selection and recommendation list has been issued for the Conductor (परिचालक) recruitment conducted under Detailed Advertisement No. 22/2024 for 500 posts in the Rajasthan State Road Transport Corporation (RSRTC). Candidates who appeared for Document Verification (DV) after the written exam held on November 6, 2025, can now check their final selection status through the official result PDF.

Recruiting Department has released an official notification for RSSB Conductor Final Result 2026 Out - Check Merit List & Cut Off PDF. 
A total of **500** posts are available for this recruitment.

The Madhya Pradesh Employees Selection Board (MPESB) has released the Result for the Group 1 Sub Group 2 Combined Recruitment Test 2025 on the official website, esb.mp.gov.in. The result was declared on 3 August 2026 for posts including Manager, Quality Controller, District Senior Horticulture Development Officer, Medical Social Worker, and Assistant Statistical Officer, among others. Candidates who appeared in the written exam can now check their qualifying status.

A total of **474** posts are available for this recruitment.

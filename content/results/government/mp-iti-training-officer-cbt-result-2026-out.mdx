MP ITI Training Officer CBT Result 2026: The Madhya Pradesh Employees Selection Board (MPESB) has declared the MP ITI Training Officer (TO) CBT Result 2026 on 24th July 2026 on the official website esb.mp.gov.in. The result is for the Computer-Based Test (CBT) conducted for 1,120 ITI Training Officer posts across various Government ITIs in Madhya Pradesh. Candidates can check their qualifying status, marks obtained, and next-stage instructions by logging in with their Application Number and Date of Birth.

A total of **1120** posts are available for this recruitment.

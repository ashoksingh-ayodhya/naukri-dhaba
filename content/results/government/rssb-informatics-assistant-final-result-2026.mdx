The Rajasthan Staff Selection Board (RSSB), formerly known as RSMSSB, has released two important documents for the Informatics Assistant 2023 (Suchna Sahayak) direct recruitment on 6 August 2026: the Order of Final Recommendation after Document Verification (List No. 05) and the Meritwise List of Finally Selected Candidates after DV. Both were published on the official RSSB results page. Candidates who appeared for this recruitment and completed document verification should check both PDFs to confirm their selection status.

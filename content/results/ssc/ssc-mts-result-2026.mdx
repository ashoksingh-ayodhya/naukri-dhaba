The Staff Selection Commission (SSC) declared the SSC MTS & Havaldar Result 2026 on August 3, 2026, on the official website ssc.gov.in. This declaration covers the Multi-Tasking (Non-Technical) Staff and Havaldar (CBIC & CBN) Examination, 2025 - Computer Based Examination result. It is important to note that this result only shortlists candidates for the Physical Efficiency Test/Physical Standard Test (PET/PST) for the post of Havaldar. The final result covering both MTS and Havaldar posts has Not Yet Been Announced and will follow after PET/PST is completed.

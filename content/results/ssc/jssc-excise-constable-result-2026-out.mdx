The Jharkhand Staff Selection Commission (JSSC) released the JSSC Excise Constable Result 2026 on 28 July 2026 in the form of the JECCE-2023 Combined Merit List. This result is for candidates who appeared in the Jharkhand Excise Constable Competitive Examination (JECCE-2023) for the post of Excise Constable. The merit list has been published as a single PDF document on the official website, jssc.jharkhand.gov.in, and candidates can check their roll number, paper-wise marks, and final qualifying status in it.

The Staff Selection Commission (SSC) has declared the SSC JE 2025 Final Result on 3 August 2026. This result is for the Junior Engineer (Civil, Mechanical, Electrical) Examination 2025, conducted for recruitment to Junior Engineer posts in various central government departments. Candidates who appeared in the Tier-1 and Tier-2 exams can check their result status through the official result PDF released by SSC.

A total of **1731** posts are available for this recruitment.

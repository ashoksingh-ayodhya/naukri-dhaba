The Department of Science & Technology (DST), Government of Bihar, through the Bihar Staff Selection Commission (BSSC), has declared the Final Result for the Office Attendant (Karyalay Parichari) Recruitment 2022 (Advt. No. 02/2022). The result covers a total of 238 posts and follows the Mains Examination held on 26 May 2026. Candidates who appeared in the Mains exam can check their result using their Enrollment Number/Registration Number and Date of Birth on the official portal.

A total of **238** posts are available for this recruitment.

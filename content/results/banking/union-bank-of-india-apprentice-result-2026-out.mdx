Union Bank of India has released the result for engagement of apprentices under the Apprentices Act, 1961. Candidates who appeared for the online written test conducted by BFSI SSC on 07.06.2026 can now check their roll number in the state-wise provisional selection list. The result was declared by the Human Resources Vertical, Central Office, Mumbai on 24.07.2026.

A total of **1865** posts are available for this recruitment.

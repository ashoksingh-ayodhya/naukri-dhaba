Central Bank of India has declared the final result for the Engagement of Apprentices for FY 2026-27 on July 27, 2026. The Human Capital Management (Recruitment & Promotion Division), Central Office has released the list of provisionally shortlisted candidates as Annexure I, along with the Regional Office contact details as Annexure II. A total of 3,655 candidates have been provisionally shortlisted following the online test conducted on July 5, 2026. Shortlisted candidates must report to their allotted Regional Office on July 30 or 31, 2026 for document verification, and the apprenticeship program will commence from August 1, 2026.

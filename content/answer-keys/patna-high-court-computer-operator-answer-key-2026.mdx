The High Court of Judicature at Patna has released the provisional Model Answer Key and Candidates' Response Sheet for the Computer Operator-cum-Typist Recruitment Examination, 2026 (Advertisement No. PHC/02/2026). The notice (No. 44/R&A) was issued on 29th July 2026. The Computer Based Written Test, comprising MCQs, an English Computer Typing Test, and a Hindi Computer Typing Test, was held on 22nd July 2026 (Wednesday) in two shifts across 18 examination centres in Patna and Muzaffarpur. Candidates can now check the model answer key and their individual response and raise objections online between 30th July 2026 and 6th August 2026.

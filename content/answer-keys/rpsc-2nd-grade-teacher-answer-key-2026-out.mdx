The Rajasthan Public Service Commission (RPSC) released the RPSC 2nd Grade Teacher Answer Key 2026 on 28 July 2026 on its official website, rpsc.rajasthan.gov.in. The answer key covers the Senior Teacher (Secondary Education Department) Competitive Exam 2025 and is available subject-wise for Hindi, Social Science, General Knowledge Group-A, and General Knowledge Group-B. Candidates can download the PDF, match their responses, and estimate their probable score before the final result is declared.

The Odisha Sub-Ordinate Staff Selection Commission (OSSSC) has released the revised answer key for the written test held for the posts of Forester, Forest Guard, and Excise Constable under CRE-2025(II). The revised key was published on 24 July 2026 after the Commission reviewed all objections received against the earlier provisional key. Candidates can log in with their User ID and password on the OSSSC website to view their response sheet.

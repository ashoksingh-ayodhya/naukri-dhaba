The Madhya Pradesh Public Service Commission (MPPSC), Indore, has released the provisional answer key for the Assistant Director/Veterinary Assistant Surgeon/Veterinary Extension Officer Exam 2026 (सहायक संचालक/पशु चिकित्सा सहायक शल्यज्ञ/पशु चिकित्सा विस्तार अधिकारी परीक्षा-2026). The exam was conducted on 2 August 2026 in a single session, and the answer key was published on 5 August 2026. Candidates who appeared for the exam can now check the answer key and raise objections, if any, through the official MPPSC website.

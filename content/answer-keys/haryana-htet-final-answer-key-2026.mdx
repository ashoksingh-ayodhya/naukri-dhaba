The Board of School Education, Haryana (BSEH) has released the HTET Final Answer Key 2026 on 5 August 2026 on its official websites, bseh.org.in and htet.eapplynow.com. Candidates who appeared for the Haryana Teacher Eligibility Test on 4 and 5 July 2026 for Level 1 (PRT), Level 2 (TGT), and Level 3 (PGT) can now download the final answer key and check whether their objections against the provisional key were accepted.

The Bihar Public Service Commission (BPSC) has released the final answer key for the Assistant Environmental Scientist Written (Objective) Competitive Examination held under Advertisement No. 88/2025. The exam was conducted on 11 June 2026, and the Commission has now published subject-wise final keys for General Studies (Paper-I) and all four optional subjects - Physics, Chemistry, Life Science, and Environmental Science (Paper-II). This article explains what changed from the provisional key, which questions were deleted, and how to download and use the final answer key PDFs.

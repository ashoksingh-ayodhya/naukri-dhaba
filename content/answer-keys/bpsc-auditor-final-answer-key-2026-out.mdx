BPSC Auditor Final Answer Key 2026 has been released by the Bihar Public Service Commission (BPSC) on its official website, bpsc.bihar.gov.in. The key covers the preliminary objective examination for the post of Auditor (Bihar Panchayat Audit Service) under the Panchayati Raj Department, advertised through Advt. No. 09/2026. The exam was held on 5 July 2026 and consisted of a single General Studies paper. This is the final answer key, not a provisional one, and it has been published set-wise for Set A, Set B, Set C and Set D.

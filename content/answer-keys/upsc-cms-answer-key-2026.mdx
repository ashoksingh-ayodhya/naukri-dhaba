The Union Public Service Commission (UPSC) has released the provisional answer key for the Combined Medical Services (CMS) Examination 2026 on 5 August 2026, four days after the exam was held on 2 August 2026. Separate PDFs have been uploaded for Paper I (General Medicine and Paediatrics) and Paper II (Surgery, Gynaecology & Obstetrics, and Preventive & Social Medicine). Candidates who appeared for UPSC CMS 2026 can download both keys from upsc.gov.in and raise objections within the official representation window, if any.

A total of **1358** posts are available for this recruitment.

The Rajasthan Public Service Commission (RPSC) has released the RPSC 2nd Grade Teacher Answer Key 2026 for the Senior Teacher (Secondary Education Department) Competitive Exam 2025-26 on 28 July 2026. The provisional answer key is available subject-wise for Hindi, Social Science, GK Group-A, and GK Group-B on the official website, rpsc.rajasthan.gov.in. Candidates who appeared for the exam, held from 12 to 18 July 2026, can download the PDF and match their responses to estimate their probable score ahead of the final result.

The answer key is available for Set A and covers five subject papers: General English, General Studies & Aptitude, Computer Engineering, Electrical Engineering, Electronics & Communication Engineering, and Mechanical Engineering. This article gives you the complete subject-wise final answers, important notes on grace marks and alternate correct options, and guidance on what to do next.

Chhattisgarh Vyavsayik Pariksha Mandal (CG Vyapam), Raipur has released the model answer key for the PSLA26 written recruitment examination conducted for Laboratory Attendant (Prayogshala Paricharak) posts under the Chhattisgarh Environment Protection Board, Nava Raipur.

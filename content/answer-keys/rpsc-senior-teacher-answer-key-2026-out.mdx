The Rajasthan Public Service Commission (RPSC) has released the Model Answer Key for the Sr. Teacher (Sec. Edu. Deptt.) Competitive Exam 2025 on 6 August 2026, covering seven more subjects: Urdu, Sindhi, Punjabi, Mathematics, Gujarati, G.K. Group-D, and English. This is a separate, later batch from the first set of answer keys (GK Group-A, GK Group-B, Hindi, Social Science) that RPSC had already released on 28 July 2026. Candidates who appeared for this recruitment exam can download the subject-wise PDF from the official website, rpsc.rajasthan.gov.in, and match their responses ahead of the final result.

A total of **9651** posts are available for this recruitment.

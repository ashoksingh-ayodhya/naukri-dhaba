The Gujarat Public Service Commission (GPSC) has declared the final answer key for the recruitment examination of Chief Scientific Officer, Class-1 (GWSSB) and Scientific Officer, Class-2 (GWSSB), covered under Advertisement No. 106/2025-26 and 130/2025-26. The written examination was held on 20 May 2026, and the final answer key was released on 30 July 2026 from Gandhinagar. GWSSB stands for the Gujarat Water Supply and Sewerage Board, the recruiting department for these two posts.
